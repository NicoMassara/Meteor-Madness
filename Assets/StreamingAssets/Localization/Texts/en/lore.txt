After decades of testing atomic bombs over sea, land, air, and even on people, humanity went further than ever before—detonating them in outer space. What began as a small experiment would ultimately become their doom.\n\nAfter a failed launch, one bomb drifted directly toward Jupiter, Earth’s natural shield. It imploded into a dazzling cloud of space dust visible from Earth. But after that beautiful explosion, everything changed. Meteors began passing near Earth more frequently than ever. In response, humanity united to build a colossal meteor-defense shield capable of instantly destroying incoming threats. Unfortunately, there was only enough material to construct part of it.\n\nHarnessing Earth’s electric field, one man—a true HERO—controls the shield, intercepting any meteor or asteroid on a collision course. His mission: protect and save Earth, sometimes aided by mysterious UFOs. And all of this… simply because humanity couldn’t resist playing with bombs again.
